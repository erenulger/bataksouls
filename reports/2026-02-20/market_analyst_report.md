## Executive Summary

BatakSouls occupies a genuine market gap: the **trick-taking card game with soulslike progression** sub-genre has no direct competitors. Card-based roguelikes are a proven category (Slay the Spire, Monster Train, Inscryption), but none use authentic trick-taking mechanics (Batak/Spades-style bidding + trump). This is both the biggest opportunity and the biggest risk — players unfamiliar with trick-taking may have a high learning curve.

**Market Opportunity Score: 7/10**

## Target Audience Analysis

### Primary Audience
- **Age:** 20–35
- **Profile:** Core PC gamers who enjoy strategy card games, Dark Souls fans open to new mechanics
- **Motivations:** Mastery (Achievers), Exploration (Explorers)
- **Platform Preference:** PC (Steam primary)

### Secondary Audience
- **Tabletop gamers** who play Batak, Spades, or Hearts and want a digital equivalent with progression
- **Soulslike fans** looking for turn-based alternatives to action games

### Audience Risk
Trick-taking is a **less-known genre** in Western video gaming. Onboarding and tutorial design will be critical to prevent player drop-off. The game needs to communicate its unique mechanics clearly on the Steam page.

## Market Opportunities

### Gap 1: Trick-Taking × RPG
No digital game offers authentic bidding + trump mechanics wrapped in RPG progression. The closest is bridge/spades apps — but none have AI personalities, HP systems, or soulslike theming.
- **Opportunity size:** Moderate niche ($500K–$2M first year realistic)
- **Competition:** Low
- **Fit:** Excellent

### Gap 2: Soulslike × Card Game (Non-Deckbuilder)
All existing soulslike card games are deck-builders. BatakSouls' fixed-deck approach (you curate a collection, not build a run deck) is mechanically distinct.
- **Opportunity size:** Small-medium
- **Fit:** Good

### Gap 3: Dark Souls Aesthetic in Card Games
Dark Souls fans are underserved in the card game space. Inscryption proves dark aesthetic drives card game sales. BatakSouls can lean heavily into lore and atmosphere.
