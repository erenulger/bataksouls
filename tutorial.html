BatakSouls

How to Play

Overview

BatakSouls is a card-based combat game inspired by the trick-taking card game Batak. You navigate a world map, encounter enemies, and defeat them through a series of card duels. Souls earned from victories can be spent at the Forge to strengthen your deck.

Combat is resolved card by card across a series of tricks. Higher power wins each trick — but an element system, a blind bidding phase, and a panic mechanic add depth to every decision.

The World

Move your character across the map using the arrow keys or WASD. The world contains enemies and a Forge.

EntityWhat happens
EnemyWalk into them to start combat. Defeated enemies stay dead until you start a new game.
ForgeOpens the upgrade screen where you can spend souls to level up your cards.

Your position and defeated enemies are saved automatically. Use Continue from the main menu to resume where you left off.

Combat

Combat is structured as a sequence of rounds. Each round has two phases:

  1. Bid Phase — Everyone secretly picks one card. The result sets the Trump element for the round.
  2. Trick Phase — 12 tricks are played. Each trick, every combatant plays one card. The card with the highest final power wins the trick and deals damage to opponents.

Combat ends when all enemies are eliminated (Victory) or you are eliminated (Defeat).

Bidding

At the start of each round, all combatants are dealt a hand of 13 cards. Then every combatant secretly picks one card from their hand to sacrifice to the Bonfire.

Once all bids are in, the element with the highest total power across all bid cards becomes the Trump element for this round. Bid cards are discarded.

Tip
The player who bid the most trump cards has their Panic reduced by 20. Winning the bid is worth doing even if your Trump card is weak.
Strategy
If you own many cards of the same element, sacrificing one of them gives you a better chance of making that element Trump — boosting all your remaining cards of that type this round.

Tricks

After bidding, 12 tricks are played. Each trick, every combatant plays one card from their hand simultaneously. The card with the highest effective power wins the trick.

The trick winner's Panic increases by +5 (see Panic). Damage is dealt to all cross-team pairs based on the power difference between their cards (see Damage).

Important
Play order is determined by Panic. Higher Panic = plays earlier in the trick. Playing earlier is a disadvantage — opponents see your card and can react to it.

Power Calculation

A card's effective power in a trick is computed from several components:

Base Power + Level Bonus + Trump Bonus + Element Bonus + Team Buff
ComponentValueCondition
Base Power3–10Set when the card is created, shown on the card
Level Bonus+2 per levelEach upgrade level adds 2 permanent power
Trump Bonus+4Card element matches the Trump element this round
Element Bonus+4 / −4Your element beats / is beaten by an opponent's element (first interaction). Subsequent interactions with the same card: ±1
Team Buff+2A teammate plays the same element in the same trick
Example
You play a Fire card with base power 7, level 2, and Fire is Trump this round. An enemy plays Magic (Fire beats Magic).

Power = 7 + (2×2) + 4 trump + 4 element = 19

Damage

After every trick, damage is applied to each cross-team pair of combatants. For each enemy/ally pairing:

  • The combatant with higher effective power deals damage to the other.
  • Damage = power difference between the two cards.
  • If both cards have equal power, no damage is dealt.
Example
You play a card with effective power 14. The enemy plays power 9.
You deal 5 damage to the enemy. The enemy deals nothing to you.

A combatant reaching 0 HP is eliminated from the fight. Combat ends when all enemies or all allies reach 0 HP.

Endurance

If a combatant runs out of cards before the round's 12 tricks are complete, the Endurance phase triggers:

SituationResult
One team completely out of cards, other team still has cards Depleted team takes damage equal to the average effective power of the opponent's remaining cards. Round ends early.
Both teams completely out of cards Everyone is reshuffled and the round continues.
Only some players depleted (mixed hand states) Depleted players take endurance damage and are reshuffled individually. Round continues.

Panic

Every combatant has a Panic value. Higher Panic means you play your card earlier in each trick — which is a disadvantage because opponents can see your card and choose counters.

Winning a trick+5 Panic
Winning the Bid−20 Panic
Panic floor10
Tip
Winning too many tricks in a row drives your panic up, forcing you to lead future tricks — telegraphing your element to enemies. Bid well to keep your panic under control.

Elements

There are 9 elements arranged in two circular weakness wheels. Each element beats exactly one other. When your element beats an opponent's element in the same trick, you gain +4 power (and they lose −4).

Mystical Wheel
Light → beats → Dark
Dark → beats → Magic
Magic → beats → Fire
Fire → beats → Poison
Poison → beats → Bleed
Bleed → beats → Light
Physical Wheel
Armor → beats → Slash
Slash → beats → Pierce
Pierce → beats → Armor
Note
The two wheels are independent. A Mystical element never beats a Physical element and vice versa.

The Forge

Souls dropped by defeated enemies can be spent at the Forge to upgrade your cards. Each upgrade level adds +2 permanent power to a card.

Power per level+2

Tip
Early upgrades are cheap. Concentrate souls on a few key cards rather than spreading upgrades thin — a level 5 card is significantly stronger than five level 1 cards.

Tips

  • Check the element wheels displayed during combat. Always know what your Trump element beats and what beats it.
  • Bid a card whose element you have many of — it turns your whole hand into Trump cards for the round.
  • High Panic is a liability. Win the Bid to keep your Panic low and preserve your ability to play reactively.
  • The damage system rewards outlasting your opponent card-by-card. A single high-power Trump card can delete an enemy in one trick.
  • Upgrade cards you use every round. A maxed-out card (+20 power) is a near-guaranteed trick win against any opposition.
  • Defeated enemies do not respawn. Clear the map before visiting the Forge for maximum souls.
